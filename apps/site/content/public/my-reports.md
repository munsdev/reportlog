---
title: My reports
description: What a reporter can do after filing: see their reports, correct, withdraw, erase their address, change their permissions, and see who opened their details.
---

A reporter who gave an email address signs in with it, with a code emailed to them, and finds every report they filed under **My reports**. A report filed through a form that asked for no address cannot be found here: nothing ties it to anybody.

## What a reporter can do

| Action                    | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Correct**               | Files a new version of the report in the same thread. The original is kept. A correction can take back permission to publish, never give it.                                                                     |
| **Withdraw**              | Takes the report off the public board. It stays sealed in its chain, and the withdrawal is recorded. A moderator never undoes a reporter's own withdrawal.                                                       |
| **Erase my address**      | Destroys the key the address was locked with, so nobody can read it again, the site included. This also withdraws the report. It cannot be undone. The same is possible from the link in the confirmation email. |
| **Permissions**           | Switch on or off: passing contact details on to somebody who asks to follow up, publishing the files, and being asked for evidence. Each is off until switched on.                                               |
| **Who opened my details** | Every time staff opened the address, a file's original, or used the address to ask for evidence: when, and the reason they gave. Never the staff member's name.                                                  |
| **A signed declaration**  | A printable declaration of the report under penalty of perjury (28 U.S.C. § 1746), with its fingerprint. The name and place typed into it stay in the browser; they are never sent to the site.                  |

## Evidence requests

If a moderator asks for evidence you said you have, the email links to a page showing only the request and the report's reference. Files sent there are added to the report as a new version. You can stop such requests by switching off being asked for evidence.
