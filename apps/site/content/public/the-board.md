## Published reports

The **board** lists published reports, newest first. Readers can search, and filter by logbook and then by the questions the site chose to make filters. There is no built-in date or place filter; a site that wants one makes its date or place question a filter. Every search and filter stays in the address, so a view can be shared.

A report's page shows its answers, its origin (filed directly, or through an organization), an editorial note if a moderator wrote one, its published files, and a link to check it.

## Numbers, activity and maps

- **Numbers**: published counts by logbook and by question. Never a bare total.
- **Activity**: counts by origin, place and hour; declined reports by reason; and every use of the flood switches and the counts-only setting.
- **Maps**: heat maps by place, where the site placed them. A place with fewer reports than the map's threshold is shown with no number.

Some logbooks are published as **counts only**: the numbers appear, the reports do not. See [Maps and numbers](/docs/admin/maps-and-numbers/).

## A site that does not publish

A site can choose not to publish a public archive at all. Then every public page and the public API say "This site does not publish a public archive", and nothing else.

## Downloads and data

Where the site allows it, the published reports can be downloaded as a spreadsheet (CSV), and each logbook's published chain file can be downloaded for checking. The counts are available to other software through the [public API](/docs/reference/public-api/).

## Email updates and the press page

Signed-in readers can have newly published reports emailed to them hourly or weekly. A site may also have a **press** page with documents it prepared for journalists.
