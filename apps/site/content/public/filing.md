---
title: Filing a report
description: What a reporter is asked, in what order, when an email address is needed, and what happens after Send.
---

This page describes what every ReportLog site does. What a particular site asks, and what it does with reports, is its operator's: its own About page and privacy policy say so.

## Choosing where to file

The front page has a button for every open logbook. `/submit` asks the same question on one card: what are you reporting?

## The cards

A report is filed one card at a time. One card asks one question, and nothing moves on by itself: you press Next. The browser's Back button goes back one card.

1. **An opening card**: which logbook this is, what happens next, and what to do if somebody is in danger right now.
2. **One card per question**, in the order the site set. A question that depends on an earlier answer appears as the next card only when it applies.
3. **The publication card.** Usually it asks whether the report may be published once a moderator approves it; a report whose reporter says no is never published. On a logbook that exists to be public, it tells you instead that filing there means it may be published. Either way, the exact wording you saw is recorded with your report.
4. **Your email address**, only when the form needs one (below).
5. **Files**, only when the logbook takes them.
6. **Check your answers**, and send.

While you fill it in, a small check runs in the background; Send works once it is done, usually in under a second. Nothing about you is collected for it, and nothing is sent to any other site. If the site turns a form away, it says so in one sentence, and sending again is all it takes.

## When you are asked for an email address

- **When the form lets you type** (a written account, a name, a link, a place that is not a list), **takes files**, or asks whether you have evidence. The report is filed only when you open the link emailed to you, within 24 hours. That link also lets you erase your address later. Afterwards you can sign in with the same address to see the report, correct it or withdraw it. See [My reports](/docs/public/my-reports/).
- **When the form is choices only**, it does not ask. The report is filed the moment you send it, with no confirmation and no receipt, and nothing ties it to you: you cannot come back to correct or withdraw it.

A site can ask for an address on a choices-only form anyway, if it wants to be able to reach reporters.

Your address is never part of the report and is never published. It is encrypted and kept apart. See [Reporter details](/docs/moderate/reporter-details/).

## After Send

- The report is **sealed**: fingerprinted and chained to the report before it. It can never be edited or deleted; a correction is a new version.
- A **moderator** reads it before anything is published.
- It is published on the board only if it was approved and you allowed it.

A site with a tips list may offer, after you send, to send a link as a tip as well. The place and time from your report are copied into the tip form in your own browser only; nothing links the tip to the report.
