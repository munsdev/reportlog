title: Organizations and intake
description: Groups whose coordinators file accounts they collected from other people, and the public directory of organizations.

An **organization** is a group, a hotline or a partner, whose people file reports they collected from others: a paper form, a phone call, an account given in person. Its people hold organization roles alongside the site-wide ones:

- **Manager**: runs the organization's page, invites people, and turns its directory listing on or off.
- **Coordinator**: files accounts for the organization.

Everybody in an organization proves a second factor, like every other role holder.

## Making one

Under **People and access › Organizations**, Access Managers and administrators make an organization with at least one manager, and give it a description and a logo. People are added by email invitation: the link lasts seven days, can be resent or withdrawn, and has no decline button (an invitation nobody takes up simply lapses). When a manager invites another manager, the invitation waits for an administrator's approval. Administrators can also add people directly, and remove them.

**Volunteer links**, under **Your organization › Volunteer links**, let a manager hand out one link that people use to join as coordinators: with a limit on how many may use it, and a switch to turn it off. The link is shown once; only a fingerprint of it is kept.

## Filing on somebody's behalf

Coordinators work under **Your organization › Intake**. They enter accounts in batches, attach each account's files, see what each one is still missing, and submit with an attestation that the account is what the person gave them. An incomplete account stays staged until it is finished.

A report filed this way seals where it came from, so a rename later cannot change what it says:

- that it was filed by a third party;
- the organization's name as it was at filing;
- how the account was collected: a paper form, a phone call, in person, an email or message, or somewhere else;
- when the coordinator attested it, and optionally when the account was collected.

The coordinator's own identity is deliberately not sealed. On the board the report carries an origin label (yours to word, under Wording) that tells readers it came through an organization. A coordinator corrects an account they filed as their own attested act, as a new version.

## The directory

The public **Organizations** page lists the organizations whose managers chose to be listed, ranked by number of members only, with a fixed note that listing is not verification: nobody has checked these organizations against a standard. A manager or an administrator turns an organization's listing on or off.
