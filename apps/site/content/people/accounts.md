---
title: Accounts
description: What an account is, email updates, and deleting your own account.
---

An account is an email address and what goes with it: a role if the person has one, their second factor, their organizations, their email updates. Anybody who signs in has one; a reporter who confirmed a report can sign in with the same address to see it.

## Email updates

Anybody signed in can ask for email updates under **Email updates**: hourly or weekly, about every logbook or chosen ones. Each email lists the reports published since the last. Every email has a link to stop them, which works for 90 days without signing in.

## Deleting your own account

At the foot of **Your account**, anybody can delete their account. It removes their role, flags, sign-in, second factor, organization memberships and email updates. A record that the account existed stays, so the audit trail still makes sense.

What stays:

- **Reports they filed.** They are sealed and cannot be deleted. Their address on those reports can be erased separately, from My reports, before deleting the account.
- **Staff audit records** about what they did as staff.

The owner cannot delete their own account; they hand the seat over first. The last manager of an organization cannot either, until somebody else manages it. Anybody with a role confirms with their second factor.
