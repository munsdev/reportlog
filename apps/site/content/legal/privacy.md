## What this website does not do

- It sets **no cookies**.
- It uses **no analytics and no tracking**.
- It loads **nothing from any other site**: fonts, images and the search are served from this website. Search runs entirely in your browser; what you type is never sent anywhere.
- It **stores nothing** in your browser.

## Hosting

This website is served by **Cloudflare, Inc.**, 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, through its Cloudflare Pages service. To deliver a page, Cloudflare necessarily processes your IP address and the details of your request (the page, the time, your browser's identification), and keeps them briefly in its logs for running and protecting its service. We do not receive these logs and do not use them.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering this website reliably and securely (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Cloudflare is based in the United States and is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, on which the transfer of data to the United States is based (Article 45 GDPR).

The address `reportlog.org` without `www` only forwards you to `www.reportlog.org`. That forwarding is done by **Hetzner Online GmbH**, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, whose web server processes your IP address and request in the same way, for the same reason and on the same legal basis, under a data processing agreement.

## Links to other sites

Links to the source code lead to **GitHub**, run by GitHub, Inc. in the United States. Nothing is sent to GitHub until you follow such a link; from then on, GitHub's own privacy statement applies.

## If you email us

If you write to us, we use your address and your message to answer you, and keep them only as long as the conversation needs (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in answering you).

## Your rights

You have the right to ask what information about you we hold (Article 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Article 16), erased (Article 17) or its use restricted (Article 18), to receive it in a portable form (Article 20), and to object to its use (Article 21). Write to the address above.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live, where you work, or where you believe the infringement took place (Article 77 GDPR).

## ReportLog sites

This policy covers this website only. Each ReportLog site is run by its own operator, who decides what it collects and publishes its own privacy policy. The documentation on this website describes what the software does with data, under [Data and privacy](/docs/reference/data-and-privacy/).
