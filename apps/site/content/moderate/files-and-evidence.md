## Files

Moderators and administrators decide files under **Moderation › Files**: a queue of files nobody has decided yet, and every file of a report from its reference. Each file is decided on its own, separately from the report:

- **Publish**: needs a short text description for people who cannot see it, and a reason. A file can be published only when the report itself is approved, the reporter allowed the report to be published, and the reporter has switched on publishing their files. The reporter's consent is the ceiling.
- **Withhold**: kept with the report, not published.
- **Refuse**: a decision, with its reason recorded, that the file will not be published.

What is published is never the file the reporter sent: it is the re-encoded copy. Withholding or refusing a published file takes it down at once.

Looking at the re-encoded copy, with its metadata removed, is free. Opening the **original**, which is evidence, needs a reason and is recorded in the access log, like opening a reporter's details.

If the reporter switches off publishing their files, files already published are taken down.

## Asking for evidence

A logbook can ask reporters whether they have evidence they are not uploading (the [evidence declaration](/docs/reference/question-kinds/) question). A moderator can then **ask for it** by email, from the report's files, when both:

- the reporter's sealed answer says they have something, and
- the reporter has switched on being asked for evidence.

The message is required. The reporter gets a link to a page that shows the request and the report's reference, and nothing else. They upload files there, wait until each says it is ready, and send: the files are added to the report as a new version, and the link is then spent. A request can be withdrawn before it is answered. Using the reporter's address to ask is recorded in the access log, and the message itself is forgotten after a while.
