## Tip links

Tips are links people send through a tips list: posts, articles or video about something that happened. Under **Moderation › Tip links**, every distinct link appears once, with the tips that sent it. A moderator marks each link:

- **Checked**: it is what it says; it goes to the journalist desk with how many tips sent it.
- **Withheld**: kept from the desk.
- **Illegal**: erases every copy of the link from every tip. It cannot be undone, and it requires the hotline procedure on the screen to be followed.

A moderator can add a comment for journalists, with the full history kept. A **blocklist** turns away exact addresses or whole domains in future tips.

## List review

Entries in every list are reviewed under **Moderation › List review**, in batches: approve or reject only the entries named on the screen, so nothing that arrived while you were reading is decided by accident. Rejecting deletes nothing. Links whose host is not in the operator's list of known hosts (`LIST_KNOWN_HOSTS`) are marked for a closer look. Triage marks what has been dealt with: new, looked at, acted on, closed.

## Illegal content notices

Anybody can tell the site that something it published is illegal, at `/illegal-content`, without an account. Giving an address is optional; it gets the sender a receipt, a link to follow the notice, and the decision by email.

Notices are decided under **Moderation › Illegal content notices**, open ones first. Every decision needs a reason, which the sender is told if they gave an address. Taking the report down is a separate act, on the report: a withdrawal, which is recorded. The report stays sealed in its chain.
