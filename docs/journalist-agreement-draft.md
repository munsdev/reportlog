# Journalist agreement (draft)

A draft for the owner to edit and publish at **Access → Journalists →
Agreement** once the site is running. Nothing here is in force until it is
published there. Every journalist accepts the published version before the
desk opens, and publishing a new version closes the desk to each of them
until they accept it again.

---

## Before you use the desk

The desk shows you links that people sent us and that a moderator has
checked, with the context they gave (where, when, what kind of thing). It
also shows the sources behind reports we have published. By continuing you
agree to the following.

1. **This is a lead, not a finding.** A checked link means a moderator
   looked at it and found it relevant and not unlawful to show you. It does
   not mean we have verified what it shows. Do your own reporting before
   you publish anything based on it.

2. **Do not present it as our verdict.** You may say a link was sent to
   this site. Do not describe it as confirmed, verified or endorsed by us.

3. **Do not try to identify the people who sent links.** We do not show you
   who they are and you agree not to try to find out, contact them through
   anything you saw here, or help anybody else to.

4. **No bulk copying.** Read the desk as a reporter would. Do not scrape
   it, copy it wholesale, or republish it as a list or dataset.

5. **Keep your access to yourself.** Your account is for you alone. Do not
   share your sign-in or show the desk to anybody who has not been approved.

6. **What we record.** Each time you open a page of the desk we record that
   you did, and which page. We keep this so we can show who saw what.

7. **Access can end.** We may close your access at any time, and it closes
   if you leave the journalist alert list. You can ask us to delete the
   details you sent with your request; see the privacy page.

8. **Questions and problems.** If something on the desk looks wrong,
   unlawful or dangerous, tell us through the contact page.
