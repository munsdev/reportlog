Style guide

Every colour, size and component the site is built from, drawn with the classes that draw it. Change the palette or the theme in the bar above and watch the whole page follow: nothing here has a colour or a shape of its own.

Palette owns colour

Gold is the action you came to take. Teal means a moderator confirmed something and crimson means serious — those two are status, never decoration and never a button.

Theme owns shape

Type, borders, shadows, radii and the width of the page. A theme sets no colour at all, so a palette and a theme can be picked separately and the pair is predictable.

The layer owns neither

client-first.css names no colour and no face. It is structure, spacing, type scale and a few components, named the way Finsweet's Client-First convention names them.

Spacing

Eleven steps and no more. A gap of 1.25rem where the scale offers 1rem and 1.5rem is a value somebody typed by eye, and the next one will be 1.3rem.

Buttons

Gold carries dark text, always — white on gold measures 2.08:1 and fails outright. Status colour never becomes a button.

button · is-secondary · is-quiet · is-solid · is-small · is-large · [disabled] — in a button-group

Every one of them shows a focus outline when you reach it with a keyboard — try tabbing through — and every one is at least 44px tall except is-small, which is the Change link beside an answer rather than something anybody hunts for.
